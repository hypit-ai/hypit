# Semantic understanding and composition

[Hypit Skill](../skills/hypit/SKILL.md); exact tool and package behavior lives with its owners.
The current architecture work is recorded separately in
[Semantics-driven composition](semantics-driven-composition.md).

Completed implementation steps, superseded status claims, temporary evidence paths, test-run
inventories and duplicated command references have been removed. Their removal does not declare
every production experience validated. New implementation work and its open questions belong in
the current design record or the affected package.

## The production objective

Hypit helps an Agent direct and produce a video. A reference supplies creative evidence: what holds
attention, how a claim becomes convincing, how performance and graphics cooperate, and which details
give the piece its character. The Agent connects that evidence to the user's actual request.

A request to clone commonly means an adaptation: use my face, use my product, change the argument,
use another language, or make a variant. Those changes can affect the Script, references, number of
examples, layout and timing. The Brief determines which relationships to retain and how closely to
reconstruct their form.

High-level understanding and exact execution reinforce each other. A vague account cannot direct a
distinctive result. A collection of accurate pixels cannot by itself explain what the new work needs.

## Why the earlier organization failed

The historical route organized work around isolated shot descriptions, then used repeated reports
of differences to guide revisions. That lost the connected model of the whole piece.

Camera boundaries do not determine the lifetime of a visual system. Independent descriptions of
one continuing graphic can produce several slightly different components. A continuity rule states
the desired result without repairing that mistaken unit of understanding. The useful model is one
system with a lifetime, state changes and relationships to the words and surrounding composition.

Repeated verbal accounts can disagree or reinforce an incorrect premise. The source must remain
easy to reopen at the relevant time and scale. A disputed overlap needs a legible view of its
geometry and surrounding frames; another confident description alone does not settle it.

An Agent that delegates interpretation loses the basis for choosing what matters and adapting it
to the user's product. Tools should make the material accessible. The Agent owns the connected
reading and the production decisions.

Searching for every difference supplies no useful end condition, especially after a swap changes
the message. A numerical iteration limit would stop activity without explaining whether the piece
works. Composition review needs a purpose tied to the target's expression.

## The director's working disposition

The creative sensibility should fit the work: social fluency, warmth, restraint, theatricality or
wit can all belong. Groundedness, care, curiosity, imagination, taste and honesty remain constant.

Understanding the whole calls for insight. Close reading calls for care and honest description.
An unexplained choice calls for curiosity: look at surrounding words, recurring systems and later
consequences. The relevant Format or Craft can sharpen the question.

Practical diligence means recording discoveries while working. Project files carry understanding
across conversations. When new material, tools or understanding improve an earlier judgment, carry
that improvement into both the production and its notes.

## Understand meaning through concrete behavior

Follow the reference from opening to close. Understand its hook, development, payoff, audience
relationship and intended response. Identify systems that persist, recur, compare, accumulate,
replace or return.

Inspect how those ideas are made visible. An account of a designed element should connect:

- its content, meaningful wording, imagery, typography, palette and hierarchy;
- its placement and relationship to the person, other graphics and the frame;
- its entry, active behavior and exit, including distinctive path, scale, pacing and settling;
- its duration and changes in relation to words, actions and neighboring events;
- the understanding, feeling or decision those choices serve.

These are responsibilities of understanding, not a form for every sampled frame. Describe a
persistent board once and locate its consequential changes. Describe a Caption system's shared
behavior once and examine its meaningful configurations.

Naming an effect is a starting point. A fade may need an account of what fades together, what enters
underneath, and how the handoff serves the argument. Several samples of one fade remain one behavior.
Compression means finding an explanation that accurately accounts for those details.

Whole-piece and close readings revise one another. A broad grid can miss brief events between
samples. Inspect the entries, changes and exits of the distinct systems through sequences dense
enough to expose their behavior. Continue investigating an unexplained relationship when it could
change the production direction.

## Semantic time connects the account to the target

A transcript and frames are source material. Analysis and Timeline preserve the Agent's connected
understanding of what they establish.

For a spoken reference, transcription and word times give a strong starting point for locating
claims, reveals and comparisons. Interpret recognized wording with the argument, visible names and
supplied context. Unfamiliar names can be transcribed incorrectly; their spelling is not a reliable
pronunciation verdict.

For the new work, Script expresses the intended words and pronunciation. The selected performance
and its semantic Outputs supply the actual timing used in composition. Wordless work can still use
named empty Segments and meaningful actions.

Keep these time relationships distinct:

| Relationship | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Source-media time | Locate the reference evidence. |
| Target Script, Selection or Moment | State what the new behavior responds to. |
| Actual target time | Locate that relationship in the selected performance. |

A-roll establishes the semantic performance. Its picture may be full-frame, inset, cut out,
covered or repositioned. Semantic role is independent of visual ownership and stacking; the
[current architecture record](semantics-driven-composition.md) develops that separation.

## Adapt the intent

A new product changes what benefits are true, what demonstrations make sense and what proof should
be shown. A supplied face establishes an identity reference while still requiring thoughtful
casting, styling, setting and performance direction.

Retain useful relationships and reconsider their expression for the new content. The reference's
arrangement is a strong starting point where it serves the goal. Treatment records the chosen
answer. Semantic attachments follow the new Script and performance; source timestamps continue to
locate evidence about the reference.

## Establish material quality through direction

Apply the relevant Craft before authoring or adapting the requests: meaningful reference
relationships, compressed image direction, strong casting, coordinated color and space, an
appropriate voice, expressive action, intended pronunciation and measured delivery.

Image Craft and its Kit own the four-part prompt structure and fixed capture language. Voice and
video Craft own casting and performance judgments; the chosen Kit owns its exact prompt wording.
Their routes should lead authors to that knowledge while making the corresponding decision.

A specific material problem calls for understanding and improving its request before deciding on
new generation. Ordinary composition work carries produced media forward. Paid calls remain within
the user's authorized commission; account availability alone does not establish that commission.

Material generation and component authoring can proceed independently where their inputs permit it.
Once Script, prompts, references, duration and spending authority are ready, material requests can
run while independent components are written. Shared spatial intent belongs in Treatment and image
direction. The actual performance establishes how moving faces and gestures interact with graphics.

The former requirement to approve a stand-in layout before generation confused material direction,
composition and spending authorization. A component style defect belongs to the component; a
placeholder cannot establish the framing of future media. Real selected material supports the
eventual composition judgment.

## Compose toward a clear deliverable

Composition review is a focused judgment of how MG, Caption, B-roll, Typography and Effects work
with the material: the comparison reads, the person has room, the reveal lands on its word, and
the handoff carries the idea onward. Detail matters through its effect on the viewer.

Choose the view that answers the question: a component example for its own behavior, actual media
for its placement, a range render or neighboring frames for a transition, and the whole Film for
the surrounding rhythm and hierarchy. Studio offers playback, editing and semantic navigation; it
can also show the finished work to the user. Its use follows the task.

Correct the owning fact: Script for the anchor, Source or Recipe for authored relationships, the
component for behavior, and Treatment for the creative idea. Reuse completed Outputs through Runs.
A failed Build remains an execution attempt with inspectable products; a new Run and Build can use
those products.

The composition is ready when layout, readability and timing carry Brief and Treatment clearly and
compellingly. Continue when a correction will improve that expression. This is a delivery judgment,
not a standing instruction to repeat a general audit or generate more media.

Sound direction also follows intent: an accent can support a reveal, a motif can connect repeated
states, and music can carry a transition. Sound Craft and audio authoring own those choices.

## Tool and documentation ownership

Media tools expose time-located evidence without deciding its meaning. Frame sampling, readable
grids, phrase navigation and word labels help inspect behavior. Actual decoded-frame time matters
when locating nearby words. Visual-change candidates help navigation; they do not define authored
component boundaries. Exact options, formats and implementation belong in the Video CLI package.

Audit documentation through the decisions an author must make: the concept, why a choice matters,
the actual supported use, an example and the routes that make it discoverable. Mentioning a term
or passing a syntax check does not establish that the knowledge is usable. Keep one detailed owner
and update its callers when the relationship changes.

Shared Skill and Kit prose remains English. The Agent speaks the user's language. Historical
rationale stays here; production pages teach the resulting judgments and capabilities.
