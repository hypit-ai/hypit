# Treatment

A quiet navy technical field establishes the media layer. A transparent Manim scene draws a luminous orbit and
the AVIR narrow waist at the centre while a separately rendered Blender object turns at the lower right. A
restrained HTML title remains independently editable above both. A brief finishing filter resolves from muted to
full color. The piece is two seconds long: enough to expose real sequential and range execution while keeping the
example fast to render.
