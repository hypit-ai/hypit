# Treatment

Two visually distinct hosts occupy the same warm retro-tech studio world. ALICE is quick, playful and
visibly impatient with hype; BOB is dry, skeptical and increasingly convinced. Four direct-to-camera
clips alternate like an edited debate.

The ranking board enters after the opening challenge and stays present while capabilities accumulate:
mere attractive generation lands in B, character continuity in A, and editable time plus word-linked
effects land in S. The board owns row layout, persistent state and entry motion as one project
component. Its reveal times are direct component-local values written by the assembly script. Visually,
it carries forward the earlier Ranking component's strongest law: a restrained dark board, instantly
recognizable tier colors and compact cards that first establish themselves on a small stage before
settling into their row. It remains secondary to the speaker and never crosses a face.

Caption remains independently editable. Every Cue is one stable, quiet reading block; only the current
Unit changes color. ALICE uses a cyan accent, BOB uses amber, and the one-off warning Style uses a
restrained rose border rather than another dominant panel. One authored Cue is retained but disabled.
A Script word attribute switches one phrase to a contrasting serif face without creating another Cue
or component family.

The last line clears visual doubt with a compact verdict burst: “生成负责可能性，NLE 负责事实.” The
final project stays useful after every generation and alignment file is removed from the editing
session because those relationships have already been materialized.
