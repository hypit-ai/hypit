# Treatment

The video opens as a direct technical demonstration: an adult male presenter fills the portrait frame and says that Hypit can call Manim during production. On the first word “First”, three opaque animation cards appear over the continuously full-frame presenter; the mathematics card immediately enters the focused, enlarged state while the other two remain smaller.

The cards remain as a persistent visual system. Each card is an independent Manim MP4 with its own scene and internal movement: 

- mathematics shows axes, a parabola and a moving tangent; 
- machine learning shows propagation through a three-layer network and a falling Loss curve; 
- physics shows a pendulum with force and velocity vectors. 

The current card enlarges and brightens on the paragraph's leading word, while the other two remain visible as smaller, dimmed, opaque cards. The cards stay within the lower demonstration area of the portrait canvas; they do not move the presenter into a separate lower panel.

The focus changes follow the accepted English performance:
`First` starts mathematics, `Next` starts machine learning, and `Finally` starts physics. When the
closing paragraph begins on `These`, all three cards return to the small overview arrangement and
continue sampling their independent sources. No card track is cleared after physics, and no source
video carries an alpha channel.

The presenter, narration and cards are separate Film contributions. The presenter and native
Seedance audio remain continuous while the project component controls only media sampling, position,
scale, opacity, brightness and layer order.
