# Brief

Produce a short portrait English explainer showing how Hypit can use Manim during video
production. Seedance supplies the adult male presenter and native narration. Three independent
Manim scenes provide mathematical, machine-learning and physics demonstrations as ordinary visual
media in the same semantic Timeline.

The audience is creators who want to bring mathematical or technical demonstrations into
presenter-led videos. The piece should make clear that each demonstration is an independently
rendered, reusable source while Hypit controls when it appears and how it is composed with the
presenter.

The Script remains the source of spoken words and semantic Moments. The presenter remains a
continuous full-frame visual contribution. Manim owns the internal animation of each scene; the
Hypit component owns external sampling, placement, scale, opacity, brightness, stacking and
containment. The three demonstrations are authored as separate source scenes and imported as
ordinary video inputs. Exact frame positions and render metadata belong to the accepted production
assets and verification records, not to this Brief.
