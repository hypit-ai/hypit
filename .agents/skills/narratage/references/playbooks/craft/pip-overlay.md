# Picture-in-picture overlay craft

- Decide whether PIP is real photographed video, supplied recording, or a composited subject with
  transparency. Use `media-track:Item` with `video`, `media`, or `surface`; do not call every non-text
  layer a sticker.
- Establish a stable placement Frame, crop, border/paint, and stack order. Keep the PIP clear of
  captions, faces, product labels, and the base shot's main evidence.
- A talking reaction PIP must remain visually alive but silent unless its audio is explicitly included.
  If it speaks, author its audio and timing as a real track rather than relying on implied lip movement.
- Use a source with alpha when the subject must float without a rectangular background. Inspect edges,
  hair, motion blur, and color spill against the actual base before accepting it.
- Time entry/exit to a perceptible story event or spoken phrase. A PIP that persists across shots must
  preserve identity, scale, placement, and styling unless a deliberate transition is authored.
