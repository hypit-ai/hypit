# ASMR format

Evidence level: thin.

- Use close, simple, predictable physical actions: cutting, pouring, wiping, pressing, folding, or
  rubbing. Keep motion micro/readable, not energetic or impact-heavy.
- Keep the shot continuous and camera close/locked. Rapid cuts or large camera moves break the
  sound-image continuity.
- Generated visuals remain silent. Author texture sound, ambience, and music as explicit
  `audio:Track`/`audio:Clip` contributions aligned to the visible event.
- Choose references with strong material texture and clean geometry. Review looping, contact points,
  duplicated tools, and end-frame artifacts before adding sound.
- Use a higher-quality generation model when fine surface detail is the point; verify the requested
  resolution is actually supported rather than assuming an upscale.
